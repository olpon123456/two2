Echoes of the Tide

Arthur adjusted his wool coat against the biting, salty wind as he walked along. He had tended the isolated Blackwood Beacon for over forty long, quiet years now. He was accustomed to finding regular driftwood, tangled seaweed, and dead crabs on shores. Tonight, however, a bright luminescent blue trail caught his keen, experienced eye immediately. The tide had deposited hundreds of spiral shells in a remarkably straight line ahead. Upon closer inspection, the alignment was definitely not an accident of natural forces.

The shells were arranged to spell out the word "RUN" in large letters. Arthur frowned, his flashlight beam dancing across the glowing, wet calcium structures on sand. The light emitted from the shells pulsed in sync with the distant ocean waves. He looked out at the dark, churning water but saw absolutely nothing unusual. The sea was calm beneath a thick blanket of heavy autumn fog today. He used his heavy boot to scatter the shells back into the surf.

The following night, the glowing phenomenon returned with even greater intensity and size layout. This time, the iridescent shells spelled out a specific coordinate and a time. Arthur copied the numbers into his pocket leather notebook with shaking, cold fingers. He returned to the lighthouse tower and tracked the location on his nautical charts. The coordinates pointed to a treacherous reef known locally as the Devil's Teeth. Yet, the ocean seemed to be insisting that something critical was happening.

Precisely at midnight, a brilliant crimson flare burst into the sky near the reef. Arthur gasped and immediately activated the lighthouse's massive, rotating emergency spotlight beam out. The powerful light pierced the thick fog, revealing a small, listing fishing vessel nearby. Its hull was pinned against the sharp rocks, taking on water very rapidly. He realized the glowing shells were a warning system generated by the sea itself. He ran down the winding stone stairs to launch his rescue boat.

The rescue was perilous, but he managed to pull three freezing fishermen aboard safely. They explained that their navigation instruments had mysteriously failed completely a few hours ago. They had been drifting blindly toward the rocks without any way to radio. Arthur steered his boat back to the safety of the lighthouse harbor. As they walked up the sandy beach, the fishermen stopped in absolute amazement. The glowing shells had formed a final message in the damp sand text.

Arthur provided the men with hot coffee, dry blankets, and warm beds inside. He sat by the window until dawn, watching the tide slowly recede away. The glowing shells lost their luster as the first morning sun arrived completely. He knew the ocean possessed deep secrets mankind would never truly comprehend at all. He felt a newfound respect for the restless, unpredictable waters outside his door. From that night on, he always checked the shore before lighting the lantern.
